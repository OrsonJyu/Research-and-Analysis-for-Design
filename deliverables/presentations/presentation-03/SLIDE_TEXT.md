## Slide 06

[Open source slide](https://www.figma.com/slides/bSGG8578Ew7Ko9BkLcZiw8/Pre-1028?node-id=4-81)

Fieldwork Workflow: From Walking to Interview

Capture the walk, build observation logs, then revisit selected moments

DURING THE WALK

Participant: Navigates naturally with a microphone to capture spontaneous speech.

Researcher: Records video and makes voice memos of observed actions and context.

01  Transcribe and review

Transcribe the researcher’s voice memos, manually check the text, and organise observation logs.

02  Interview with video support

Use selected clips and logged events in semi-structured interviews to discuss decisions and information use.

RECORD: Walk + audio + video ↓ ORGANISE: Reviewed observation logs ↓ REVISIT: Participant explanations

Researcher voice memos are fieldnotes. They are not participant think-aloud data.

## Slide 08

[Open source slide](https://www.figma.com/slides/bSGG8578Ew7Ko9BkLcZiw8/Pre-1028?node-id=7-2)

Initial Data Organisation

Descriptive action tags for observation logs — not yet the qualitative codebook

S  STOP  Visible halt in stepping

P  PHONE_VIEW  Discernible viewing toward the phone

I  PHONE_INPUT  Visible phone touch, swipe or input

H  HEAD_TURN  Visible change in head direction

G  SIGN_VIEW  Viewing an identifiable sign

Q  HELP_REQUEST  Audible or reliably documented request

R  RESEARCHER_PROMPT  Researcher instruction or intervention

T  RESEARCHER_TALK

Talking with the researcher; unclear content is marked T?.

K  KIOSK_USE

Viewing or typing on a public electronic directory; not coded as I.

T and K: working codes added during fieldwork, unified across all five cases.

Q is not inferred from a backward look or showing a phone. R is a researcher prompt; T is conversation with the researcher.

Data Filtering: Video Memo Coding

Re-describing observed segments from voice-memo logs, checked against video and interviews

HOW SEGMENTS WERE FILTERED

01 Voice-memo observation logs

Start from the researcher’s transcribed voice memos: the observation log of each walk.

02 Check against the video

Review each logged moment in the recording; confirm, correct or add what the memo missed.

03 Corroborate with interviews

Use semi-structured interview accounts to support or question the segment, where available.

04 Re-describe with code combinations

Rewrite each selected segment as a code sequence, e.g. S + P → S + H, with location, situation and environment.

Data Processing

Prepare traceable information-interaction events for later qualitative analysis

PRIMARY: Observation logs — Build the event record

VERIFY: Video — Check actions and timing

CONTEXT: GPS — Support location and route

Observation: What was seen or reliably recorded? Action, time, place and information source.

Participant Account: What did the participant say? Link the explanation to the relevant moment.

Interpretation: What might the evidence mean? Keep the inference and its support explicit.

EVENT RECORD   =   context + information source + action sequence + linked account

Challenges and Adjustments

Make the record more usable without claiming more than the evidence supports

01 Writing while walking is difficult → Use researcher voice memos, then organise them into observation logs.

02 Transcription needs checking → Manually review transcripts and uncertain passages; use video for verification.

03 Kiosk use and researcher talk add actions → Unify them as T and K, then back-check earlier records (G* / D* → K).

04 Behaviour does not explain its own meaning → Do not infer being lost or burdened from stopping or looking at a phone alone.

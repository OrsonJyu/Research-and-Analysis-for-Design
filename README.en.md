# Research and Analysis for Design

**SD5008 · PolyU ISD · Safari · Repository v0.4.0 · 28 October 2026**

This repository preserves the Safari team's research process, literature, decisions and deliverables for **Attention Allocation and Navigation Information Design for Newcomers to Hong Kong**. The project examines how information supports orientation, action and confirmation during unfamiliar walking journeys, and when it is associated with interaction burden.

Start with the [project state](docs/PROJECT_STATE.md), [research brief](docs/RESEARCH_BRIEF.md) and [document index](docs/INDEX.md). Agents should read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before continuing work.

## Current working baseline

The route is **PolyU South Gate → K11 Art Mall as a mandatory waypoint → the Barbour storefront at K11 MUSEA, 3F, Shop 319**. Passing Art Mall does not end the task. See the [route record](docs/fieldwork/ROUTE.md) and [current execution pack](deliverables/fieldwork/extended-route-v3/README.md).

The supplied manual specifies one researcher following one participant; primary fieldnotes; auxiliary neck-mounted GoPro recording for verification and episode recall; and Hong Kong clock times. Its seven shorthand action labels are mapped to earlier labels in the [codebook crosswalk](docs/fieldwork/CODEBOOK_CROSSWALK.md).

The route is confirmed as the team's plan. Fieldwork began on 27 October 2026: raw media for P01 and P02 (27 Oct) and P04 and P05 (28 Oct) are on Google Drive/iCloud. Video-first analysis reports exist for [P01](docs/fieldwork/sessions/P01_2026-10-27_video-first-report.md), [P02 with cross-case comparison](docs/fieldwork/sessions/P02_2026-10-27_video-first-report.md) and [P04 Tom](docs/fieldwork/sessions/P04_2026-10-28_video-first-report.md) (with [filtered episode coding](docs/fieldwork/sessions/P04_video_coding.md)). Teammates' post-walk interviews for P01 and P02, the English coding and interview analysis for P03 (Peter) and the researcher memo for P05 (Chenggong) are now archived; a [cross-case note](docs/fieldwork/CROSS_CASE_NOTES.md) summarises all five cases. All await team review and are not findings. Completed v3 record forms, full interviews for P04/P05 and the contents of ethics approval have not been supplied. The manual's approximately 28 minutes / 1.9 km is a planning estimate; indoor store-finding time remains to be checked. Blank forms and illustrative events are not collected data.

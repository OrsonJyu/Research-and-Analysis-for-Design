G* and D*

G* extends viewing to an electronic mall directory; D* is proposed DIRECTORY_INPUT. Both are retained for shared team scope agreement.

A plus sign joins behaviours supported together. An arrow marks a change across the row: for example S + P → P. Where a parent record contains several phases, the code column lists the relevant combinations and the details give their exact subwindows. A question mark qualifies only the uncertain component, such as S + P?.

Long records are review windows and may contain several shorter actions. H and isolated input samples are not treated as continuously active for every instant of a broad window. Linked details keep the original IDs and are not additional independent episodes.

Timing and review: file-relative timestamps are the primary index. HKT values are approximate camera-metadata conversions. Full time ranges were reviewed at two-second frame intervals with 0.5–1 second checks around candidates; the additions below are separately identified. Audio-based R candidates and actor attribution retain their review status.

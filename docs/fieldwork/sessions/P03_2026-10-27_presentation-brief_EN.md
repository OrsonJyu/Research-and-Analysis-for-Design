6 Filtering language for questions from the tutor

We use the term data selection and quality control rather than calling participant accounts dirty data. Our decisions depend on the analysis: assisted footage can describe behaviour, but independent performance is analysed separately; perceived vibration is valid experience data, while the device's actual output needs separate verification. We preserve the raw material and keep a decision log.

No. They are evidence about experience and decision logic. We distinguish them from verified phone or GPS events.

The codebook defines observable evidence. Provisional labels are kept separate from confirmed labels, and context flags determine which comparisons a segment can support.
